Remainder

Partial refunds to everyone who paid, in proportion, exact to the cent, sent once. Through PayPal.

PayPal sandbox only. The league, the families and every amount are made up.

1 The season

Twenty-four families paid this season's registration through PayPal. Fees differ: $120 per player, $108 for a second sibling, $60 on a scholarship. One family, the Okafors, was already refunded in full when they moved.

2 What the treasurer wrote

Claude reads the notice and writes down only what it says, each item with the exact sentence it came from. The code checks that every quote is really in the notice and that every amount is in its quote. Claude never works out anyone's refund.

3 The refund plan

Each family's share is the pool times what they paid toward registration, divided by what all included families paid. Everyone gets the whole cents of their exact share. The cents left over go one each to the largest fractions; ties go to whoever paid first.

4 Approve and send

Each refund carries its own PayPal-Request-Id, fixed when the plan was made. If the server dies after PayPal made a refund but before we wrote it down, sending again with the same ID gets back the refund PayPal already made instead of a second one.

The process died after PayPal made refund #9 and before it was written down. One row is stuck in SENDING: we can't tell from our own books whether that family was paid.

5 Check against PayPal

Reads every order back from PayPal and compares PayPal's refunds with the ledger, family by family.

6 The same crash, without request IDs

A second season with the same families, the same notice and the same plan, sent the usual way: no PayPal-Request-Id, and a plain retry after the crash.
